The Coupled Financial Infrastructure Failure & Systemic Risk Simulator models how operational failures in financial infrastructure can propagate into institutional liquidity stress and broader financial contagion.

Using PostgreSQL and Neo4j, the system represents infrastructure dependencies and inter-institutional exposure networks to simulate cascading failures across both operational and financial layers.

The platform identifies critical nodes, traces probable failure-propagation paths, measures systemic risk, and evaluates possible mitigation strategies to reduce the impact of cascading disruptions.
